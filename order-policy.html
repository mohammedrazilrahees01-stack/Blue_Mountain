Order policy

Effective date: 1 January 2026

Policy overviewBack to top ↑Visit store ↗

At BuyHoreca, we are committed to delivering a seamless and dependable ordering experience for hospitality professionals and businesses. This Order Policy outlines the process from order placement to delivery.

01

Placing an Order

Orders may be placed through our website or directly with our customer support team.

We cater to both individual and bulk requirements, including large-scale hospitality and

commercial kitchen projects.

02

Order Confirmation

Once an order is placed, you will receive a confirmation and updates via WhatsApp, E-mail

or phone. Our team may reach out to verify details for larger or customized orders to

ensure accuracy.

03

Pricing & Payment

We offer flexible payment options, including:

All prices are listed in AED and may be subject to change. Delivery charges and applicable

fees will be communicated at checkout or during order confirmation.

  • Cash on Delivery
  • Card Payments
04

Order Modifications & Cancellations

We understand that requirements may evolve. Orders can be modified or canceled by

contacting our team prior to dispatch.

Once an order has been processed for delivery, changes may no longer be possible.

05

Delivery & Timelines

We work with trusted logistics partners to ensure timely and secure delivery.

  • Standard delivery across the UAE is completed within 1–2 working days
  • Next-day delivery is available for most orders
  • International shipping is available, with timelines varying based on destination
06

Order Tracking

To keep you informed at, we provide order updates via:

  • WhatsApp notifications
  • E-mail
  • Direct communication from our team
07

Delivery Requirements

Please ensure that:

Delays due to incorrect information or unavailability at the delivery address may impact

timelines.

  • Delivery details are accurate and complete
  • Someone is available to receive the order at the specified location
08

Bulk & Project Orders

For bulk purchases, commercial kitchen setups, or project-based requirements, our team

offers tailored support, including quotations and coordinated delivery schedules.

09

Customer Support

Our team is available to assist you throughout the ordering process, from product selection

to post-delivery support.

You may reach us via phone, email, or WhatsApp for prompt assistance.
